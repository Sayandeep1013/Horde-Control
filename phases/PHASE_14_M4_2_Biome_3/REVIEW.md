## The bar

A phase meets its bar when all of the following hold at once:

- Phase execution score at least 8/10.
- Every task score at least 7/10.
- No open Blocker or Major finding in `LEDGER.md`.
- Every exit test listed in `PLAN.md` > Exit criteria and acceptance tests is passing.

Three failed review iterations stop the loop; execution does not continue to a fourth. Instead, the author receives the `LEDGER.md` summary and a diagnosis of why the bar was not reached, per `phases/README.md` loop rule (d).
