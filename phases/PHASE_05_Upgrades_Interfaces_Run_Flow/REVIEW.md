## The bar

A phase meets its bar only when all of the following hold:

- The phase execution score is at least 8/10.
- Every task score is at least 7/10.
- No Blocker or Major finding is open in LEDGER.md.
- Every exit test named in PLAN.md > "Exit criteria and acceptance tests" is passing.

If three review iterations in a row fail to reach this bar, the loop stops; the author is brought the ledger summary and the reviewers' diagnosis instead of a fourth iteration.
