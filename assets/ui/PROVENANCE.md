## Art-consistency pass (D161): project-made pixel art

All of the following is project-made, generated by `tools/art/make_pixel_icons.py` (run from the repository root; it overwrites the files below). No third-party content. Palette: the pack's ink colour (22,28,46) for the 2 px outline, flat three-tone fills, no antialiasing; UI glyphs are baked light grey and tinted at draw time. `telegraph_exclaim.png` is replaced by a 22x30 pixel "!" (the 100x100 vector one above is superseded, still decision D142's marker).

## v1.0 project-made icons and sprites (decision D182)

Generated, not third-party. 55 coloured 32x32 icons `assets/ui/icons/icon_<id>.png` (every Battle Skill, player and Tower ability, passive, Draft card, difficulty, plus dash, kills, elite, boss, burn, slow, freeze, repair, chest, meat, timer) come from `tools/art/make_pixel_icons.py` (`V1_ICONS`); review sheet `assets/ui/icons/_contact_sheet_v1.png`. World sprites in `assets/sprites/v1/` (axe, caltrop, frost_shard, lightning_segment, ballista_bolt, catapult_rock, war_chest, war_chest_open, burn_ground) come from `tools/art/make_v1_sprites.py`; review sheet `assets/sprites/v1/_contact_sheet.png`. SpriteFrames `dynamite.tres`, `explosion.tres`, `meat.tres` come from `tools/art/generate_enemy_sprite_frames.py` and point at the CC0 Tiny Swords sheets (D183).

## v1.0 Arena Interactables (WP-ARENA, decision D299)

Project-made, no third-party content. Sprites come from `tools/art/make_v1_sprites.py` (`ARENA_SPRITES`, same `compose()` pipeline as the other v1 sprites, nearest filter), icons from `tools/art/make_pixel_icons.py` (`ARENA_ICONS`); review sheets `assets/sprites/v1/_contact_sheet_arena.png` and `assets/ui/icons/_contact_sheet_arena.png`. The earlier v1 sprites and icons are byte-identical to before. Two cues, `wood_break` and `shrine_claim` (seeds 130 and 131), were added to `tools/audio/make_sfx.py` (`python tools/audio/make_sfx.py wood_break shrine_claim` writes only those two WAVs).
