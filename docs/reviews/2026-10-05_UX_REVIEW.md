# Horde Control: UX Review (2026-10-05)

Reviewer: senior UX engineer (interface experience only; balance and gameplay belong to the game-design review).
Build: `main` at 767f279, Godot 4.7.1, captured at 1280x720 (canvas_items stretch from the 1920x1080 base, `project.godot:20-22`).
Evidence: code is cited as file:line. Screenshots are in `sandbox/captures/review/` (the earlier review set), `sandbox/captures/ux/` (taken for this review) and `docs/screenshots/`. `docs/screenshots/06-results.jpg` is older than the D115 settlement rows (it has no Scrap line), so the run-end findings use the code as the authority.

This review does not say any gate is passed. The reviewers and the author decide that.

---

## 1. Verdict

The interface is skinned well and built carefully: the containers are well formed, the shape-not-colour rules are honoured, the input lockouts work and there are audio cues. What it fails to do is explain itself. A new player starts a run with no prompt at all. The screen is full of symbols the game never names: blue crystals and gold "$" sacks lying on the ground, purple or red diamonds over goblins' heads with rings under their feet, an empty circle beside "Reroll", a white tick inside the Tower bar, a level counter that reads "Level 0". Several primary actions are physically hidden. At 1280x720 the War Camp's **Settings** and **Back to Title** buttons and the title screen's **Quit** button are pushed off the bottom of the screen. The player character vanishes completely behind the Tower while still taking damage. When the Tower is off-screen and below 40% health, its indicator stops pointing at it. Each of these can be fixed in hours, not days. The bigger gap is teaching. There is no first-run guidance, the Draft never says how to pick a card, nothing says what Scrap is for, and the game never tells the player which goblin attacks whom. Fix the eight items in section 6 and a first-time player can decode every pixel on the screen.

**Cross-cutting input problems.**
1. **Hold Up to confirm** is the only confirm that works on every device. It is undocumented on the Controls panel and unlabeled on every screen that uses it: the Draft, the pause menu, run-end and Settings.
2. **Gamepad glyph wording is inconsistent**: "R / Square" in the Draft, "R, X / Square" on the Controls panel, "Space / click / A" in the Skill Tree.
3. **The left mouse button is a global "confirm"**, so a click in empty space on a choice screen confirms the current highlight.
4. **There is no D-pad movement** (project.godot:26-53), although the D-pad navigates menus.

## 4. Questions a real player asks

Each entry gives why the question matters, the factual answer with evidence, and a verdict.

1. **"What are the blue rhombuses on the ground?"**
   *Why it matters:* they are the only source of level-ups. *Answer:* XP shards (`data/pickups/xp_shard.tres:32`). Walking within 96 px pulls them in (MASTER_SDLC.md:3220). They fill the bottom bar, which is purple, not blue (`hud.gd:921`). *Verdict:* **fail**. The colours do not match and nothing teaches it.
2. **"What are the gold 'S' / '$' circles?"**
   *Why:* they are the run's currency. *Answer:* Scrap pickups drawn with Tiny Swords' gold-sack sprite (`data/pickups/scrap.tres:25`). The HUD calls them "SCRAP". *Verdict:* **fail**. The art says gold and the text says scrap.
3. **"Why do those pickups just sit there?"**
   *Why:* players assume collection is automatic. *Answer:* collection needs the 96 px magnet radius. Pickups stay still until attracted and expire after 60 s (MASTER_SDLC.md:1398, 1403). *Verdict:* **fail**. The radius is never shown.
4. **"What are the pulsing diamonds over the goblins and the circles under them?"**
   *Why:* this is the main dodge cue. *Answer:* the attack telegraph during wind-up: a `telegraph_diamond.png` marker 104 px up and a 26 px squashed ring (`telegraph_visual.gd:41-46`, `89-94`). *Verdict:* **fail**. The ring is static, its size does not match the reach, and its colour changes per enemy.
5. **"Where am I?"**

## 5. Prioritized findings

Effort: S is up to 2 hours, M is up to a day, L is more than a day. Register items are flagged where a number is involved.

### P0: blocks or misleads every new player

**P0-1. Hub menu buttons off-screen (Settings, Back to Title).**
Evidence: `ux/hub_fresh_90.png`; `hub_screen.gd:513-521` (the card is centred on a 300 px band; six buttons need about 600 px; the stale comment says "Start Run + 3 buttons").
Fix: in `_build_menu_card()`, anchor `MenuCenter` to `PRESET_CENTER_BOTTOM` with a bottom margin of `UiPalette.SCREEN_MARGIN`, growing upward (`grow_vertical = GROW_DIRECTION_BEGIN`). Or set `offset_top` to 400 and `offset_bottom` to 1050 and reduce `BUTTON_MIN_SIZE.y` from 68 to 56. Add a render test asserting that every button's rect is inside the viewport at 1280x720. Effort: **S**.

**P0-2. Title Quit button clipped.**
Evidence: `review/title_90.png`; `title_screen.gd:345-346`.
Fix: the same anchoring as P0-1, or move the castle up about 100 px. Effort: **S**.

**P0-3. Player invisible behind the Tower.**
Evidence: `ux/behind_tower_240.png` (HP 92/100, the player not visible); no occlusion code in `src/`.
Fix: in `src/tower/tower_visuals.gd`, each frame, test whether the player's `global_position` falls inside the Tower sprite's on-screen rect while the player's y is less than the Tower's y. If so, tween the Tower sprites' `modulate.a` to 0.45, and back to 1 otherwise. This is cosmetic and reads public state only, matching how `TowerVisuals` already polls. Effort: **S-M**.

**P0-4. The Draft never says how to choose; the ring is unlabeled and invisible.**
Evidence: `review/run_2400.png`, `ux/crop_reroll.png`; `draft_controller.gd:1063-1094`; `draft_fill_ring.gd:140-144`.
Fix: (a) idle centre-shape colour becomes `UiPalette.TEXT` at full alpha; (b) add a footer Label "Click · 1/2/3 · Space/A · hold W/↑ to pick" (string key `DRAFT_HOW_TO_PICK`); (c) caption the ring "Hold"; (d) put a "1/2/3" key badge on each card (`draft_card_view.gd`, header row); (e) change the reroll text to `"Reroll (%d left): R / X"`, greyed at 0. Effort: **M**.

**P0-5. No first-run teaching (attack is automatic, pickups, enemy intents, goal).**
Evidence: no tutorial or hint code in `src/run`, `src/ui` (grep); `title_screen.gd:400-412`.
Fix (the small version, buildable in a day): a `FirstRunHints` CanvasLayer that shows three timed, non-pausing toasts during wave 1, gated by a `MetaProgress` flag (`first_run_hints_seen`): "Your bow fires automatically. Move with WASD." / "Walk near crystals and gold to collect them." / "Red goblins attack the Tower, yellow ones hunt you." The bigger version, a T1-T4 teaching sequence (docs/19 mentions teaching waves), is an **author decision**. Effort: **M** (toasts).

**P0-6. The off-screen Tower marker loses its direction below 40%.**
Evidence: `threat_feedback.gd:574-580`.
Fix: always draw the arrow. Below 40%, colour it `UiPalette.DANGER` and draw the diamond as a backing badge behind the tower icon at `icon_pos`. The shape still changes (Register: "changes both shape and colour"), and the direction is kept. Effort: **S**.

### P1: confusing, but players eventually work it out

**P1-1. Pickups are undecodable and look uncollectable.**
Fix: tint `pickup_xp_crystal.png` to the XP bar colour, or set the XP bar's `fill_color` to the crystal blue (`hud.gd:921`). Add a 2 px idle bob to `pickup.gd`. During wave 1 only, draw a faint magnet ring (96 px, Register "Player body / ... / magnet radius 96") under the player. Effort: **S-M**.

Use one danger colour for every enemy. Effort: **M** (it needs a public wind-up-progress query on `EnemyController`).

**P1-5. Scrap has no visible purpose; the Scrap/Gold naming conflicts with the art.**
