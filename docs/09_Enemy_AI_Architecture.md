### Intent Behaviour Defaults

These Provisional Defaults make the three prototype intents implementable without a system document. Full stat blocks for every archetype are in the Provisional Values Register; document 09 owns their final form.

- **Attack slots.** Attack slots around a target: N = floor(2π × (target radius + attacker body radius + reach) ÷ (2 × attacker body radius + 4)), evenly spaced (Tower with Tower Seekers: 27; player with Player Hunters, reach = contact hitbox margin 6 px: 7). An enemy claims the nearest free slot when within 64 px of the slot ring; an enemy with no free slot waits 32 px outside the ring and accrues no stuck time. Steering is seek toward the claimed slot (or the target when none is claimed) plus separation from EntityRegistry neighbours within 32 px, computed inside SimLoop; the prototype uses no NavigationAgent2D.
- **Tower Seeker body-block rule.** A Seeker whose path is blocked by the player steers via the attack-slot model above. Blocked means less than 8 px of progress over 2 seconds with the player within the Seeker's 20 px reach; time blocked by the player accrues no stuck time. While blocked it attacks the player with its normal telegraphed attack (15 damage) **without changing intent**, and resumes its path the moment it is clear.
- **Player Hunter leash rule.** A leash timer starts the moment a Hunter spawns and resets every time it damages the player. If 20 seconds pass without a reset, the Hunter telegraphs for 0.5 seconds and then converts permanently into a full Tower Seeker — it adopts the Tower Seeker's complete behaviour and stat profile while keeping its current HP. This is the event-driven behaviour that resolves infinite kiting and keeps the safe-corner ban true. Finisher Player Hunters spawned during Overtime carry no leash timer, and the finisher flag survives any conversion (Wave Director › Wave Runtime Model, Overtime).
- **Opportunist event rule.** An Opportunist picks the closer of player and Tower at spawn. Tower distance is measured to the Tower's footprint edge. The out-of-range event fires after 3 continuous seconds outside the 400 px aggro range, and its timer restarts after every re-evaluation, whether or not the Opportunist switched. It re-evaluates only on three events: it takes damage from the other target; the out-of-range event fires; its current target dies. On re-evaluation it switches only if the other target is at least 25% closer than the current target and at least 3 seconds have passed since its last switch (hysteresis and a switch lockout, both Provisional Defaults).
- **Stuck rules.** Stuck timers run only while an enemy is outside attack or contact reach of its target and is not merely held back by other enemies already attacking that same target; an enemy queued behind others simply moves to the nearest free attack slot. After 3 seconds without path progress (its distance to target has shrunk by less than 8 pixels) it falls back to its secondary target or a direct approach if it has none. Prototype secondary targets: Tower Seekers and Player Hunters have none (their 3 s stuck step is direct approach); the Opportunist's is the other target. After 8 seconds of less than 8 pixels of displacement the global stuck detector forces direct approach; after 20 seconds still stuck it despawns, its drops are placed at its position, and it counts as removed, not killed, for every stat and stall calculation.
- **Telegraph minimums.** Melee wind-ups are at least 0.4 seconds; ranged wind-ups at least 0.8 seconds. Difficulty scaling never reduces these. Body contact damage is exempt from these minimums, because approaching into contact range is itself the telegraph.
